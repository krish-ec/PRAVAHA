How it works

Seven steps from scrap to a traceable sale.

01

Identify

Photograph the scrap. The app suggests a category — even offline.

02

Value

See the fair price range for your area, before you agree to sell.

03

Match

Get matched with authorized recyclers who take this material.

04

Arrange

Request a quote, then choose pickup or drop-off.

05

Handover

A QR e-waste passport is created for the handover.

06

Get paid

Cash or UPI, with an instant ledger update.

07

Trace

The full chain is recorded for EPR and compliance reporting.
